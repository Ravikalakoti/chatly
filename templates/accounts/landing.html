Questions, answered

What counts toward the daily limit?

Each text message, voice message and photo you send counts once. Receiving messages never counts, and you can read your chats as much as you like.

When does the limit reset?

Every day at {{ reset_label }}. Unused free messages don't carry over to the next day.

Do bought messages expire?

No. They stay in your account until you use them, and they're only used after your free messages for the day are gone.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled by Razorpay, so you can pay with UPI, cards or netbanking. Your messages are added as soon as the payment goes through.

Who can see my chats?

Only you and the person you're chatting with.
